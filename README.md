## Employee Data Analysis Project

## Overview

This project analyzes employee data from ABC Company to uncover insights related to team distribution, job roles, age groups, and salary patterns. The analysis includes data preprocessing, visualization, and interpretation of key trends.

---

## Objectives

* Perform data preprocessing and cleaning
* Analyze employee distribution across teams and positions
* Identify predominant age groups
* Evaluate salary expenditure by team and position
* Explore the relationship between age and salary
* Present findings using visualizations

---

## Tools & Technologies

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Jupyter Notebook

---

## Data Preprocessing

* Cleaned and structured dataset
* Replaced invalid values in the **Height** column with random values between 150–180
* Verified data consistency before analysis

---

##Analysis & Key Findings

### 1. Employee Distribution by Team

The distribution of employees across teams is largely balanced, with most teams having similar counts. A few teams such as **New Orleans Pelicans** and **Memphis Grizzlies** have slightly higher employee numbers, while teams like **Minnesota Timberwolves** and **Orlando Magic** have slightly fewer.

---

### 2. Employees by Position

Employee distribution across positions is fairly balanced, with **SG** and **PF** having slightly higher counts, while **C** has comparatively fewer employees, indicating minor variation across roles.

---

### 3. Age Group Distribution

The majority of employees fall within the **20–30 age group (~79%)**, while the remaining (~21%) are in the **30–40 range**, indicating a predominantly young workforce.

---

### 4. Salary Expenditure by Team

Salary expenditure varies across teams, with the **Cleveland Cavaliers** having the highest total salary, while teams like the **Philadelphia 76ers** have comparatively lower expenditure, highlighting differences in team payroll distribution.

---

### 5. Age vs Salary Relationship

The analysis shows a **slight positive relationship between age and salary**, where salaries tend to increase with age. However, there is noticeable variation, suggesting other factors also influence salary.

---

## Overall Insights

* Workforce distribution across teams and positions is **generally balanced**
* The organization has a **young employee base**, dominated by the 20–30 age group
* Salary spending is **uneven across teams**, indicating concentration of resources
* **Experience (age) has a positive but not strong impact on salary**
